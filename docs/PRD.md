## 1. 📌 Executive Summary & Problem Context

Coastal fishing in India supports over **4 million fishermen** across 7,516 km of coastline. However, small-craft and artisanal fishermen face existential hazards daily:
1. **Accidental Maritime Boundary Violations:** Due to sea drift, lack of intuitive navigation tools, and obscure demarcation of the International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL)—especially in the Palk Strait / Gulf of Mannar (India–Sri Lanka) and Sir Creek (India–Pakistan)—fishermen inadvertently cross sovereign borders, risking arrest, vessel impoundment, and loss of life.
2. **Fatal Weather Surges:** Localized sea-state escalations (swell waves > 2.5m, squalls > 25 knots) cause hundreds of capsize incidents annually because raw meteorological bulletins fail to reach boats in time or are incomprehensible to non-literate crews.
3. **Sub-optimal Catch Yields & Fuel Waste:** Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) advisories generated by ISRO/INCOIS (from Oceansat-3, INSAT-3DR, and MODIS Chlorophyll-a and SST gradients) are broadcast via SMS or web portals that require complex chart interpretation, leading to excessive diesel burn and reduced economic returns.
4. **Linguistic & Literacy Barriers:** Coastal communities speak distinct regional dialects (Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Odia, Gujarati, Marathi) and require low-cognitive-load, voice-first audio interaction on rugged mobile smartphones rather than complex text tables.

### The ORCA Solution
**ORCA** is an autonomous, neuro-symbolic multi-agent maritime mobile conversational assistant and navigation app built with **Flutter**. It ingests real-time Earth Observation (EO) satellite data, oceanographic forecasts, and spatial boundary vectors, passes them through deterministic safety guardrails, and synthesizes actionable voice and visual guidance in native Indian languages directly on fishermen's mobile devices.

## 3. 🚀 Core Value Propositions

1. **Zero-Hallucination Safety Guarantee:** Multi-agent LLM reasoning is strictly bounded by deterministic GIS algorithms (`Shapely`, `GeoPandas`). The LLM cannot invent coordinates or approve dangerous paths.
2. **Native Multilingual Voice-First Mobile UX:** Flutter app integrated with Bhashini APIs for continuous Speech-to-Text (ASR), Neural Machine Translation (NMT), and Text-to-Speech (TTS) in regional languages with oversized tactile controls.
3. **Dynamic Drift-Aware IMBL Geofencing:** Employs predictive 15-minute lookahead vectors based on live mobile GPS speed ($v$) and heading ($\theta$) to warn crews before physical boundary breaches occur.
4. **Obstacle- & Hazard-Avoiding Marine $A^*$ Pathfinding:** Discretizes water grids, penalizing wave heights $> 2.0\text{ m}$, restricted Marine Protected Areas (MPAs), and shallow shoals.
5. **Offline-First Resilience with Native SQLite/Hive:** Pre-voyage caching packages boundary vectors, 24-hour weather grids, and local PFZ coordinates to enable complete offline mobile functionality on deep seas with zero cellular coverage.

## 4. 📋 Functional Requirements (FR)

### FR-1: Multimodal Mobile Conversational Interface & Voice I/O
- **FR-1.1:** Flutter app shall accept audio voice queries in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, and English via an oversized push-to-talk mic button.
- **FR-1.2:** Audio shall stream to the Bhashini ASR pipeline and convert to text with latency $< 1.2\text{ s}$.
- **FR-1.3:** Output advice shall be converted to natural regional speech via Bhashini TTS with native audio playback controls and waveform visualizers.
- **FR-1.4:** Chat history shall display synchronized regional text transcriptions alongside English debug tokens for auditability.

### FR-2: Intelligent Multi-Agent Query Deconstruction
- **FR-2.1:** LangGraph orchestrator shall route user intents into specialized worker nodes (`WeatherAgent`, `PFZAgent`, `BoundaryAgent`, `RoutingAgent`).
- **FR-2.2:** Multi-hop reasoning shall support composite queries (e.g., *"Find the nearest PFZ with calm waves and give me a safe path that stays 5 km away from Sri Lanka"*).
- **FR-2.3:** System shall dynamically aggregate outputs from multiple agents into a unified structured state before synthesis.

### FR-3: Deterministic Neuro-Symbolic Safety Guardrails
- **FR-3.1:** Hard weather cutoff: If wind speed $> 25\text{ knots}$ or wave height $> 2.5\text{ m}$, system must emit a critical abort/return warning regardless of LLM generation.
- **FR-3.2:** Boundary cutoff: If distance to IMBL $< 2\text{ km}$, mobile app triggers a high-priority local emergency buzzer alarm and computes an immediate 180° retreat vector.
- **FR-3.3:** The LLM shall NEVER output synthetic geospatial coordinates; all coordinates in responses must originate from deterministic GIS modules.

### FR-4: Marine $A^*$ Pathfinding & Navigation
- **FR-4.1:** System shall generate an 8-directional collision-free LineString path between vessel GPS and target PFZ coordinates.
- **FR-4.2:** Landmass polygons (ISRO/OSM coastline), islands, coral reefs, and active storm cells shall be classified as zero-traversal obstacle cells.
- **FR-4.3:** Cost function shall incorporate distance, wave resistance, and distance penalties when navigating near boundary buffer zones.

### FR-5: Earth Observation & Marine Data Ingestion
- **FR-5.1:** Ingest real-time Open-Meteo Marine API feeds (Significant Wave Height $H_s$, Swell Wave Height, Wave Direction, Wind Speed, Sea Surface Temperature).
- **FR-5.2:** Parse INCOIS/MOSDAC Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) advisory GeoJSON/CSV data (Chlorophyll-a front boundaries, thermal gradients, validity windows).
- **FR-5.3:** Cache all ingested geospatial and weather rasters in a local TTL-governed cache (Redis/SQLite) to prevent rate-limiting.

### FR-6: Interactive Tactical Marine Mapping on Mobile
- **FR-6.1:** High-performance vector map canvas (Flutter Map / MapLibre Native) supporting high-contrast nautical dark mode and outdoor sunlight mode.
- **FR-6.2:** Layer management for:
  - Red Zone: International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL) with 2 km and 5 km dynamic buffer ribbons.
  - Green Zone: Potential Fishing Zones (PFZs) with chlorophyll concentration tags.
  - Orange/Amber Zone: High-risk weather cells ($H_s > 2.0\text{ m}$).
  - Cyan Line: Computed $A^*$ collision-free safe navigation route.
- **FR-6.3:** Native Mobile Telemetry HUD displaying GPS Lat/Lon, Heading, Speed (knots), Distance to IMBL, and Sea State Index.

### FR-7: Offline-First Pre-Voyage Caching on Mobile
- **FR-7.1:** Provide a "Pre-Voyage Pack" button allowing fishermen to download a 24-hour bounding-box bundle (weather forecast, active PFZs, coastline & boundary vectors) directly into device storage (`sqflite` / `hive`).
- **FR-7.2:** When mobile device loses connectivity at sea, the app executes local Haversine distance calculations and plays pre-cached emergency voice alerts without internet access.

## 6. 🏆 Success Metrics & KPIs for Evaluation

1. **Safety Enforcement Score (100% Target):** Zero test cases where a route intersects land, an MPA, or crosses the IMBL boundary.
2. **Fuel & Distance Optimization:** $A^*$ pathfinding achieves a $\ge 12\%$ reduction in voyage distance compared to heuristic straight-line sea-waypoints.
3. **Linguistic Accessibility:** 100% test scenario completion using voice commands in Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi without keyboard input on mobile.
4. **Offline Resilience:** Complete simulation of disconnected mobile voyage mode demonstrating persistent navigation warnings and static boundary alerts.
